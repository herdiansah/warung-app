# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus discussion on ideas, evidence, and the project.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal, customer, or credential data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized language, doxxing, and sharing private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the GitHub account [@herdiansah](https://github.com/herdiansah). Reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed fairly.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
